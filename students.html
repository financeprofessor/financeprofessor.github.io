For my students

Whether you are in one of my classes, curious about a project, or planning a PhD, this page tells you what I teach, how to reach me and what you can get involved in.

Courses

Accounting

Financial Accounting

How businesses record transactions and prepare the balance sheet, income statement and cash flow statement.

Accounting

Management Accounting

Using cost and performance information to plan, budget, price and make decisions inside a business.

Analysis

Financial Statement Analysis

Reading published accounts through ratios and cash flows to judge a company's performance and risk.

Finance

Introductory Finance

The time value of money, risk and return, and how firms and investors value assets.

Risk

Enterprise Risk Management

Identifying, measuring and managing the risks an organisation faces, across the whole business.

Also taught

Markets and fintech

Financial Markets and FinTech, along with Financial Management and Financial Modelling at other institutions.

I teach at undergraduate, MBA and PhD levels. Course outlines, readings and deadlines are shared through the university's course system for your section.

Getting help

The quickest way to reach me is by email at prashant.gupta@mahindrauniversity.edu.in. Please write from your university account and put your course name and section in the subject line.

If a topic is not clicking, come and talk to me early rather than the week before the exam. Email me and we will find a time to meet.

Projects and research

If you are curious about any of the topics below, I am glad to discuss a project or a research idea. A good first step is to read one of my papers on the Research page and come with a question.

  • Fintech and digital payments
  • Central bank digital currencies
  • Banking and asset quality
  • Corporate governance
  • Personal finance
  • Mindfulness

Thinking about a PhD?

I did my PhD in Finance at IIT Delhi, on non-performing loans in Indian banks, and I now sit on Mahindra University's PhD Selection Committee. If you are considering doctoral study, I am happy to talk through what the path involves.

On campus
