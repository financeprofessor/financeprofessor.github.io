About

I am an Assistant Professor of Accounting and Finance at the School of Management, Mahindra University, Hyderabad. I hold a PhD in Finance from IIT Delhi and have spent over a decade teaching, researching and running academic programmes at institutions including Mahindra University, IIT Delhi and NALSAR University of Law.

My research covers corporate governance, bank asset quality and central bank digital currencies. It has been published in international journals and recognised with best paper awards, and I have worked on projects funded by the Government of India.

Alongside teaching, I chair the Ranking and Accreditation Committee, lead the university's NCC unit, which I set up in 2024, and look after the boys' hostel. Outside the university I am an Art of Living teacher and run leadership and well-being programmes for students, professionals and inmates of central jails.
