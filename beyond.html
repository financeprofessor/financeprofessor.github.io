Beyond Class

Outside the lecture hall I teach well-being and leadership programmes with the Art of Living, and lead the university's NCC unit.

Art of Living

I have been an Art of Living teacher since 2011, running personal excellence, leadership and well-being programmes for young people and professionals across India. Before that I was a volunteer and trainer, and led the youth team of the Ranchi chapter for over three years.

At Mahindra University

YES!+ and the Happiness Program

Under the university's MoU with the Art of Living, I organise the YES!+ workshop for students and the Happiness Program for faculty and staff, both sponsored by the University.

Campuses

Programmes at other institutions

IIT Delhi, IIT Dhanbad, IIM Ranchi, NLU Ranchi and BIT Mesra.

Service

Programmes in central jails

Stress management and rehabilitation programmes, including meditation and yoga, at Tihar Central Jail in Delhi and Hotwar Central Jail in Ranchi.

Reach

Who takes part

Students and professionals, as well as participants from defence, banking, private security and underprivileged communities.

National Cadet Corps

I started Mahindra University's NCC unit and serve as its Founding Officer In-Charge, holding the rank of Lieutenant as an Associate NCC Officer. Our cadets have earned National Camp selections and several wins at Combined Annual Training Camps.

Sport

I played basketball for the CBSE Jharkhand state team, which won the national-level MHRAT competition organised by the DAV group.
